The joke, one line about office life:

> I automated my job with a script; now I spend eight hours a day making sure nobody notices the script.

The angle: automation was supposed to free up the day — instead the whole day goes to hiding the automation. Recognition with a turn at the end.

`main.py` (Python, stdlib only) writes exactly that one line to `entry.txt` — in `OUTPUT_DIR`/`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set, otherwise next to the script. Output is deterministic and byte-identical across runs.
